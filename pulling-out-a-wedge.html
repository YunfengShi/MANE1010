Pulling Out a Wedge

A 300 lb crate sits on a wedge of negligible weight, with its left side against a wall. The wedge rests on a slope at angle α and a horizontal force P acts on it: positive P pulls to the left, negative P pushes to the right. The same static friction coefficient μs acts at every contact. Set P, α and μs, then press Play: nothing moves, the wedge comes out and the crate drops along the wall (slipping at all four surfaces), or a large enough push drives the wedge and crate up the slope together.
