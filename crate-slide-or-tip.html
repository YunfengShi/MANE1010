Crate: Slide or Tip?

A 3 ft wide, 6 ft tall crate with its center of gravity G 3.5 ft above the floor is pushed by a horizontal force P. Find the friction force and where the resultant normal force acts, measured from the front corner A. The crate weighs W = 100 lbf. Set P, the height of P and the friction coefficient μ, then press Play to see whether the crate stays put, slides or tips. If it tips, the push lets go once the crate has rotated about 35°.
